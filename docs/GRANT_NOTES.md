# Grant Positioning – OpenForge Africa

## Problem Statement (Grant Language)

Open and Distance Learning institutions across Africa experience persistently high dropout rates driven by learner isolation, absence of structured peer interaction, and assessment models that collapse when connectivity is poor. Existing digital solutions either require continuous internet or treat learning as solitary content consumption.

## Solution Summary

OpenForge introduces offline-first Study Circles with AI facilitation and micro-assessments specifically engineered for low-connectivity, low-device environments common among African open university students.

## Alignment

- **SDG 4** (Quality Education)
- African Union Continental Education Strategy
- Digital inclusion and youth skills agendas of major funders
- Retention and learning quality metrics valued by Mastercard Foundation, IDRC, UNESCO, Ford Foundation, and related education windows

## Measurable Outcomes

- Increase in peer interaction frequency
- Improvement in continuous assessment completion rates
- Reduction in isolation-related dropout indicators
- Tutor time saved through digest-style oversight
- Device-level evidence of offline engagement

## Pilot Readiness

Designed for rapid pilot with NOUN and potential expansion to other open universities in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and beyond.
